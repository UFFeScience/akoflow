A workflow can finish an activity on one machine and need its output on another. In this AkôFlow fixture, a payload traveled from the PlaFRIM Slurm environment to Kind, then to a GCP worker, and finally back to PlaFRIM. The last activity checked the original payload and both intermediary receipts before writing its final receipt.

<!-- truncate -->

## Follow each handoff

| Environment | What the activity verifies or writes |
| --- | --- |
| PlaFRIM source | Writes the payload and its SHA-256 checksum. |
| Kind | Verifies the payload and checksum, then writes its receipt. |
| GCP | Verifies the incoming files and writes the cloud receipt. |
| PlaFRIM destination | Checks the payload and both receipts, then writes the final receipt. |

The recorded execution completed **four activities** and reported **391 bytes** across three transfers. Its observed makespan was **545.536 seconds**, including the distributed execution lifecycle. The GCP instance was destroyed after the run. The small payload makes this a check of routing and integrity.

## Use the fixture to check your topology

The [round-trip source bundle](https://github.com/UFFeScience/akoflow-examples/tree/main/real/plafrim-kind-cloud-roundtrip) includes the scope, network, workflow, and plan. Adapt the environment IDs and capacity to your deployment. After a run, check the output receipt alongside the activity and transfer records so you can confirm where the files traveled and whether they arrived intact.
